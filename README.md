## Nomenclature for individual signals

### Song (vocalization)

Per Payne and McVay (1971) and many others since, song nomenclature includes: 

1. a *song session* is a period in which a song is sung once or more;
2. a *song* is a repeated sequences of themes;
3. a *theme* each a sequence of phrases;
4. a *phrase* is a sequence of units;
5. a *unit* is an individual signal made up of *sub-units* (per Figure 1 of Payne and McVay, 1971):

### Non-song vocalization

12 signals commonly heard within the Salish Sea and Johnstone Strait (inland waters of Washington State, USA, and British Columbia, Canada) are shown as spectrograms with playable corresponding audio clips in the [Salish Sea Humpback Vocalization Catalogue project](https://www.orcasound.net/portfolio/humpback-catalogue/) (v1 built by Emily Vierling in 2022:

The 12 signal types in version 1.0 of this humpback signal dictionary are:

1. Whup
2. Grunt
3. Ascending Moan
4. Descending Moan
5. Moan
6. Upsweep
7. Trumpet
8. Growl
9. Creak
10. Buzz
11. Shriek
12. Chirp

[~9000 calls annotated with these labels are available as open training data within the orcadata wiki](https://github.com/orcasound/orcadata/wiki/Other-training-data:-humpback-whales#an-open-collaborative-humpback-signal-catalogue-catalog).
